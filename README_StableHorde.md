# AI Horde Image Generation

- To generate images, use the [on-site generator](https://aihorde.net/generate/image) or an app from the
  [list of integrations](https://aihorde.net/guis). The [image generation guide](https://aihorde.net/docs/image) walks
  through a first image.
- To share your GPU, see [Contribute your GPU](README.md#contribute-your-gpu-become-a-worker) in the main readme.
